# Rules for outreach

_The owner edits this. The copywriter and the compliance reviewer both read it._

## Tone

- Plain and specific. Short sentences. No hype, no fake urgency.
- First message under 120 words for email, under 60 for WhatsApp.
- One ask per message.

## Every message must

- Say who it is from, matching `sender_identity` in `desk.json`.
- Say why this person is being contacted, using something true about them.
- For email: end with an opt-out line, such as "Reply STOP to opt out."

## Never

- Claim a result, customer or number that is not in `brain/offer.md`.
- Imply a prior relationship that does not exist.
- Use "Re:" or "Fwd:" on a first message, or any misleading subject.
- Contact anyone on `suppression.md`, or anyone who asked us to stop.

## Sequence

- At most 3 messages per lead per channel, at least 3 working days apart.
- Stop the sequence the moment a lead replies. A person handles it from there.

## Law

Rules on cold email, WhatsApp and telemarketing differ by country and are the
owner's responsibility. Write the rules that apply to you here (for example
consent requirements, do-not-call registries, required sender details) so the
team follows them.
